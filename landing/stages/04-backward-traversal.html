Backward traversal

Start where it hurts — the manifest symptom sm — and walk the graph backwards. Everything that cannot reach the crash is pruned, no matter how anomalous it looks. What remains is the causal anomaly set: the only suspects allowed in the room.

module backward_traversal.py closure Anc(s_m) = ancestors ∪ {s_m} cost O(V+E) · <10 ms verdicts 100% deterministic

↳ What it does

Shrink the suspect list with math

An anomaly off the causal path is a coincidence, not a cause. The closure separates the two.

closure + pruning on a 9-step tracesvg
the dashed walk is the only direction blame is allowed to travel

↳ How it works

Two phases, one invariant

Phase one finds the symptom; phase two finds everyone who could have caused it.

  1. a
    Locate the symptom sm

    The latest step with score < 0.7 or anomaly_detected = True — the visible crash, not the verdict.

  2. b
    Compute the closure

    Anc(s_m) = nx.ancestors(G, s_m) ∪ {s_m} — every node with a directed path into the symptom, in O(V+E).

  3. c
    Intersect with anomalies

    C_anom = anomalies ∩ Anc(s_m). Peripheral warnings like s9 and downstream noise like s18 are pruned even though the judge flagged them.
