TraceInspector

The turn-by-turn autopsy table. Every step becomes a card carrying its agent, recipient, action payload, tool response, and — once judged — a health score with the judge's finding. Cards are stamped ROOT CAUSE ORIGIN, INHERITED SYMPTOM, or VALID TURN, and stay in two-way sync with the graph: click either side, both sides follow.

↳ Interactions

Graph and log, one selection

A single selectedStepIndex drives both panes.

  1. a
    Scan the pills

    Red ROOT CAUSE ORIGIN, amber INHERITED SYMPTOM, green VALID TURN — the whole trace triaged at a glance.

  2. b
    Open the evidence

    Each card shows the raw action payload plus the environment / tool response state, then the judge's score and textual finding for that exact turn.

  3. c
    Click to teleport

    Selecting a graph node smooth-scrolls its card to the center of the list — and clicking a card highlights its node. Ground-truth blame, where present, rides along as a pill.
