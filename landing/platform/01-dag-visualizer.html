DagVisualizer

The causal graph you can touch. A hierarchical vis-network canvas renders every step as a color-coded node — red box for the root cause, amber for inherited symptoms, emerald for clean turns. Clicking a node selects the step everywhere: inspector scrolls to it, tooltips show its judge score and reason.

↳ Interactions

Hover to suspect, click to convict

Every visual choice maps to a verdict field — nothing decorative.

  1. a
    Read the colors

    Root renders as a large red box with a glow shadow; symptoms are amber dots; clean steps emerald; pruned branches slate. Selected nodes get a cyan border.

  2. b
    Follow the red edges

    Edges on the causal_dependency_chain — or sourced at the decisive step — draw red and thick; the rest draw thin blue. Variable labels name the shared identifier.

  3. c
    Click to cross-examine

    Clicking a node fires onSelectStep: the TraceInspector auto-scrolls to that turn with its payload, tool output, and judge score.
